Privacy

Last updated: 2026-05-22

Jay's Air Center respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

We use anonymous, cookieless analytics to understand how this site is used. Specifically, we record:

  • Pages viewed and sections scrolled into view
  • Clicks on call-to-action buttons and contact links
  • Form starts and completions
  • Approximate country (from your IP, which is then discarded), device type, and viewport size
  • An anonymous random identifier stored in your browser's local storage so we can tell a returning visitor apart from a new one

We do not use cookies. We do not store your IP address. We do not share any of this data with third parties. The data lives in our own infrastructure.

What we collect when you fill out our form

Information you voluntarily provide through our contact form (name, email, phone, aircraft details, message) is sent directly to our team and is used only to respond to your inquiry. It is not shared.

Your choices

To opt out of the anonymous analytics, you can clear your browser's local storage for this site, or block our analytics endpoint with a browser extension. This will not affect site functionality.
