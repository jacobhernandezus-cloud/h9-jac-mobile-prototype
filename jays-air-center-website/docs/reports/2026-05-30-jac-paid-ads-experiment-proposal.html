$500 Paid Social Experiment

A short, contained paid-ads experiment to see whether we can profitably acquire qualified hangar and FBO inquiries through Instagram/Facebook ads, on top of the organic traffic already arriving at jaysaircenter.com.

The Short Version

What: Spend $500 over 3 weeks running Instagram and Facebook ads targeted to high-net-worth pilots and aircraft owners within 50 miles of SNA. Drive them to jaysaircenter.com.

Why now: The CTA experiment running organically has shown the funnel works (4 real form completions in week 1) and we have qualified industry referrers (NBAA, OCair.com, AirNav) already finding us. Paid is the natural next step to test whether we can buy more of the same.

Realistic outcome: 100+ button clicks and 10+ form completions from the $500, with a pipeline value somewhere in the $540K to $1M+ range based on aviation lifetime-value math.

What we need from you: 15 minutes to react to this proposal, ~1 hour to set up a Meta Business account for JAC, and willingness to handle 10-50 inquiries arriving in your inbox over 3 weeks.

Why Try This Now

The CTA experiment that launched 5/23 has already produced four real form submissions and surfaced excellent organic referrers — NBAA.org, OCair.com, AirNav.com — which means the right audience is already finding the site. The question worth answering before that organic experiment closes on June 6: can we buy more of this exact traffic profitably?

$500 is a small enough check that even a disappointing result doesn't hurt. But it's large enough to generate a meaningful sample size in 3 weeks. And paid traffic gives us another lever — once we know what creative and audience converts, we can choose to scale anywhere from $0 to whatever budget we want.

The Plan — 3 Phases Over 3 Weeks

  • Days 1-7
    Audience discovery
    Find out who clicks. Run 3 ad sets in parallel with the same creative but different targeting. Test broad aviation interest vs. narrow HNW homeowner vs. lookalike audience. Pick the winner by cost-per-click.
    $150
  • Days 8-14
    Creative iteration
    Find out what works. Take the winning audience from Phase 1. Test 3 different ad creatives — single image, multi-image carousel, short Reel video. Pick the winner by cost-per-click and form-completion rate.
    $200
  • Days 15-21
    Scale + lookalike
    Validate scalability. Take the winning audience + creative combo. Add a lookalike audience seeded from form submissions so far. Run at $50/day for 3 days. Final read-out at end of week 3.
    $150

Total: $500 hard cap. $50 reserved unspent throughout for any "boost the winner" push in the final week.

What You Will Notice

During the 3 weeks of the experiment, two things change for you operationally:

  • Inquiry volume increases. Realistic case: 10–25 form submissions in your inbox over 3 weeks (so 1–2 per business day on average, with bursts). Stretch case: 25–40. Plan response capacity accordingly.
  • Inquiry mix shifts toward awareness-stage prospects. Organic visitors today self-select — they're already researching hangar space. Paid visitors may be earlier in the buying conversation. The conversation may need to start with "let me explain what we offer" rather than "let me tell you about availability."

The live dashboard at jaysaircenter.com/dashboard separates paid from organic traffic so we can see the difference in real time. The credentials Daniel sent you earlier still work; the dashboard refreshes on every page load.

What This Doesn't Change

  • The current organic CTA experiment continues unchanged. Paid traffic enters the same 3-variant rotation as organic, so we still get clean variant comparison data for both audiences.
  • The form path to your inbox is unchanged. Inquiries still flow through formsubmit.co to george@jaysaircenter.com exactly as today.
  • The website's look and feel doesn't change. No on-site work required for this experiment; only off-site ads.
  • JAC's brand voice is unchanged. Ad copy stays in the same premium, understated register as the site itself.
