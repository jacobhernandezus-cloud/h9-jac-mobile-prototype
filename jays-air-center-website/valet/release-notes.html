What this is

Jay's Valet lets an aircraft owner or tenant summon their plane like a Tesla: request ramp staging or schedule an arrival from the app, the line operator stages it on the ramp and valets the customer's cars (or meets the aircraft on arrival and parks it), and the customer is notified at every step. Scope is limited to ramp parking and staging — no hangar pull-in/out or towing. This page summarizes current scope, key decisions, and what's next.

Who uses it — three personas

Personas define who can do what.

✈️ Owner

Based owner with a ramp tie-down. Full customer experience, billed to account.

  • Request ramp staging & arrivals
  • Cars to valet & services
  • Tipping + history

🧑‍✈️ Line operator

Jay's ground staff who stage and park aircraft on the ramp and valet customers' cars.

  • Work the request queue
  • Advance status
  • Receive tips

🔑 Tenant

Leases a ramp tie-down. Self-registers in-app, billed per use.

  • In-app sign-up
  • Same request flows as owner
  • Scoped to their aircraft

Key flows

Departure — Request ramp staging

Scheduled only, 2-hour minimum lead (keeps last-minute ramp traffic down).

Request→Operator assigned→Staging on ramp→Cars valeted→Staged & ready

Arrival — Schedule & Announce

Schedule an ETA (~2 hrs out), then announce on final. Services requested before parking.

Announce→Dispatched→Meeting aircraft→Services→Parked on ramp

Two versions to compare

Version A — Concierge

Full flow plus an electronic tip screen after the operator completes the job. 100% to the line operator.

Version B — Standard

Identical flow, no tipping. Ends with a simple rating / thank-you.

In the prototype, the tipping toggle at the top switches between A and B live.

Decisions in this iteration rev 3

Scope limited to ramp parking & staging

Removed all hangar functionality — pull-in/out and towing services are out. The app is strictly a ramp staging & valet service.

"Cars to valet" added to the staging request

Tenants specify how many cars they'll park in the staging area, so the valet knows how many vehicles to handle.

Fuel deferred to a later phase

No fuel features for now; fuel-related feedback is parked for a future iteration.

Three personas formalized

Owner, line operator, tenant — each with a defined feature set.

Departures are scheduled only

2-hour minimum lead; removed "stage now" to prevent last-minute ramp congestion.

Tenant self-sign-up

Renters onboard themselves in-app, billed per use.
