JAY'S RAMP VALET

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Jay's Ramp Valet ("the app") is operated by Jay's Air Center, 2980 Airway Avenue, Costa Mesa, CA 92626. It lets tenants of Jay's Air Center schedule aircraft arrivals and departures, request fuel, and tip the line crew. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, aircraft tail number, aircraft type, and lease location. You provide these when you create an account.
  • Service requests: the arrivals, departures, fuel orders, notes, tips, and ratings you submit, along with timestamps and the crew member who handled them.
  • Payment information: if you tip by card, payment is processed by Stripe. Your card number never touches our servers; we store only the tip amount and Stripe's payment reference.

What we don't collect

  • No location tracking, no advertising identifiers, no analytics SDKs, no contact list access, no photos.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

How we use it

Solely to operate the valet service: dispatching the line crew to your aircraft, billing services to your tenant account, paying tips through to crew, and showing you your request history.

Where it lives

Data is stored with our hosting provider (Netlify, Inc.) in the United States. Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes. Sessions expire automatically after 30 days.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data or deletion of your account at any time by contacting us. Deleting your account removes your profile and unlinks your request history.
