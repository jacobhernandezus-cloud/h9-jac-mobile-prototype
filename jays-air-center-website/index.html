About Us

A New Chapter in
Orange County Aviation

Jay's Air Center is an entirely new concept and alternative for general aviation aircraft owners and operators in Orange County. It was designed for busy executives, mobile entrepreneurs, and the members of the general aviation community that value prestigious facilities at John Wayne Airport. It features superior design and architecture, offers unmatched privacy and security, and built with sustainability in mind.

Aircraft Hangars

More Than Storage.

Your aircraft deserves a home that honors its craftsmanship and protects your investment. Our hangars are a sanctuary for your aircraft and come with 24/7 security, white-glove maintenance coordination, privacy, and climate control options.

  • 24 Hour web-accessible surveillance camera system
  • Optional diamond polished hangar floors
  • Energy-efficient skylights and clerestory windows providing natural light
  • Optional energy efficient Big Ass Fans
  • Fire Sprinklers
  • Wired for high-speed internet
  • Electrical supply to produce adequate power for ground equipment

Tie Downs and Ramp Parking
